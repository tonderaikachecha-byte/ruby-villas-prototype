The experiences

Feel the Zimbabwean welcome.

From the traditional table to moments by the water, discover the experiences that give Ruby Villas its character.

Made to remember

There's more to a place than what you see.

It's in the way food is shared, conversations unfold, and the day takes a different turn. These are the experiences that make a visit feel personal.

01 / The Zimbabwean table

Come hungry.
Leave connected.

Enjoy locally rooted flavours under the thatch. Traditional Zimbabwean cuisine is one of the ways Ruby Villas brings guests closer to the culture and warmth of home.

02 / By the water

Let the day slow down.

The dam-side setting and advertised boat cruise experience offer a change of pace. Ask the team about boat availability and what a visit can include.

03 / Explore

Take the scenic route.

For those who like to move, Ruby Villas also advertises quad bike experiences. Availability, safety arrangements and suitability should be checked with the team.

04 / Gather

Some moments deserve a setting of their own.

Ruby Villas accommodates celebrations and group gatherings. Tell the team what you are planning so they can confirm options and arrangements.
