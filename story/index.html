Our story

A dream brought home.

The story of a journey, an idea and one founder’s determination to create something distinctive in Zimbabwe.

The beginning

Some journeys bring you back to yourself.

While travelling in Tanzania, Enock stayed somewhere that left a lasting impression. He was captivated by the idea of a place built around its own cultural character and the experience it gave visitors.

He began to imagine creating something with that spirit back in Zimbabwe. Drawing on his own connection to rural Zimbabwe and a belief in the country's hospitality, he set about turning that idea into Ruby Villas.

The result is a property that reflects his vision: a place to stay, to gather and to experience the warmth and traditions of Zimbabwe in a more personal way.

This is an editorial retelling of Enock's account, to be reviewed and approved by him before public use.
